🌱 Raitha Bandava

Raitha Bandava is an AI-powered agriculture platform built to help Karnataka farmers get useful farming information in a simple and accessible way.

Features
🤖 AI Chatbot – Get agriculture-related guidance in English or Kannada.
🌿 Disease Detector – Upload or capture a crop image to identify diseases and get recommendations.
🌾 Crop Recommendation – Get crop suggestions based on district, soil type, and season.
📅 Crop Calendar – Access crop-related recommendations.
🚜 Smart Farming – Learn about modern farming techniques.
🏛️ Government Schemes – Explore agricultural schemes and services.
🔊 Kannada Voice Support – Listen to disease results in Kannada.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Backend: Python, Flask
AI/ML: TensorFlow, EfficientNetB0
AI Chatbot: Groq LLM
Database: SQLite
Deployment: Railway

🎯 Goal
The goal of Raitha Bandava is simple: use AI and technology to make farming information easier for Karnataka farmers to understand and use.
